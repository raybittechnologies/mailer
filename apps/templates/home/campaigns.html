Unsubscribe contact upon reply

***IMPORTANT PLEASE READ CAREFULLY***

PROFILE TAB > Unsubscribe Contact Upon Reply > (DEFAULT = ON)

What It Does:
When this is ENABLED (Default!), anytime someone responds to your campaign (even if it's an auto-response), that email & all emails associated with that venue will be automatically unsubscribed from future follow-up emails in that sequence.

When it’s DISABLED, venues replying to your emails won’t get unsubscribed from the follow-up sequence — the contact will continue receiving the rest of the scheduled emails unless you manually unsubscribe them from the campaign.

Why You Might Want to Leave It ON:
  • If someone responds but you forget to manually unsubscribe them, it can get awkward — especially if they already said “not interested”, or worse, are already considering booking you, but end up still getting ANOTHER follow-up email later.
  • Be honest with yourself — if you’re busy and not always on top of checking every reply right away and unsubscribing contacts — you want to leave this on (NO NEED TO CHANGE ANYTHING / DEFAULT = ON).
  • The only downside is, all the people who are interested in booking you — you will have to keep following up with them (if necessary) manually every time until you get booked.
  • You can always seamlessly subscribe them back by toggling the SUBSCRIBED button.

NOTE: ANYTIME someone responds to an email a reminder will be created, so don't worry — your leads won't fall through the cracks.

Why You Might Want to Turn It OFF:
  • If you’re great at keeping track of replies and unsubscribing people manually, this setting may not be necessary.
  • It’s helpful to switch it “off” in cases where someone replies positively (e.g., “What’s your pricing?”).
  • Then you reply back, but if they don’t respond again, the automated follow-ups will still go out — which can help you close the deal.
  • This prevents ghosting or you getting bogged down by committing to weekly follow-up emails manually, since they are still subscribed to the auto-emailing system.
